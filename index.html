Win chances come from checking every possible way the bracket can still finish, treating each remaining match as a 50/50 coin flip. Now is a player's locked-in points; max is the most they could still reach if every result fell their way. A player is eliminated only when they finish top in none of those possible brackets — a mathematical certainty, not just a long shot.
